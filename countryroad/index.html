Country Road — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

The short version: Country Road is an offline app. Everything you record — trips, maintenance, fuel, odometer, setup profiles, documents, photos — stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and we never collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

1. Who we are

Country Road is a motorcycle trip and maintenance tracker developed by Gianluca Piccolo. For any privacy question, contact: gianlucapiccolo21@gmail.com.

2. Data the app stores (locally only)

All data you enter is saved exclusively in the app's private storage on your device: planned routes and notes, maintenance and expense records, odometer readings, fuel fill-ups, bike & rider setup profiles, chain/tire wear measurements, seasonal checklists, document photos (insurance, registration, license, etc.), maintenance photos, and GPS ride logs. None of this is ever uploaded anywhere by the app.

3. Location

Location is used only while you actively start a ride with the optional GPS Ride Tracker (off by default, enabled in Settings). Positions are processed on your device to compute distance and duration, stored locally in your ride log, and never transmitted. Tracking stops when you press stop. You can deny or revoke the location permission at any time; the rest of the app works fully without it.

4. Camera and photos

The camera and photo gallery are used only when you choose to attach a photo to a maintenance record or save a document to the Document Vault. Images are copied into the app's private storage on your device and are not transmitted anywhere.

5. Network requests (no personal data)

The app is otherwise offline, with two narrow exceptions, both anonymous:

Map thumbnails: route preview images are loaded as standard map tiles from CartoCDN (OpenStreetMap-based). Link resolution: when you save a Google Maps short link, the app fetches that link to read its coordinates. Like any web request, these expose your IP address to the respective service (Carto / Google), but the app sends no personal data, no identifiers, and nothing you have recorded.

6. Notifications

Maintenance reminders are local notifications scheduled entirely on your device. No push servers are involved.

7. Backups and sharing

You can manually export your data as a JSON file and share or store it wherever you choose (email, cloud drive, etc.). Exported files are under your control and your responsibility; the app itself never sends them anywhere.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual entries in-app at any time, and uninstalling the app permanently removes all stored data and photos.

9. Third parties, analytics, advertising

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no social plugins, and no third-party data sharing of any kind.

10. Children

Country Road is a general-audience utility app and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change (for example, an optional cloud backup feature), this policy will be updated here and the effective date revised before such a feature ships.
